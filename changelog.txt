[h2]0.20.0[/h2]
[b]Version numbers start again from 0.x:[/b] the old 1.0.0 to 1.9.1 are now 0.10.0 to 0.19.1. Full changelog: [url=https://github.com/EtienneBinginot/Terraria-Ultimate-Randomizer/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md]CHANGELOG.md[/url]
[list]
[*][b]Kill jackpots:[/b] every enemy kill has its own small chance of a jackpot, whatever the drop style (setting "Jackpot per kill", 1.2 % by default, less on weak enemies). About one jackpot every 30 minutes early in the game; before the Wall of Flesh, rarely a hardmode item.
[*]Items in jackpot chests get the jackpot effects, sound and chat message when they are first picked up.
[*]Jackpots far above your progression are rarer, so that more jackpots do not mean hardmode items in the first hour.
[*][b]Expert and Master:[/b] the codex no longer lists a boss's normal-mode-only drops, which come in its treasure bag instead. Nothing was unobtainable: the bag always held the same randomized items.
[/list]
