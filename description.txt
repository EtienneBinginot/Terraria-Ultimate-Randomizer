==[Ultimate Randomizer]==
A complete, configurable randomizer for Terraria, tied to each world.

Current features:
- Random enemy and boss drops, random treasure bags, crates and goodie bags
- Coherent mode: items keep their category and tier, and unlock as you defeat bosses (no Zenith from the first slime... unless you are very lucky)
- Guaranteed shuffle mode: every item is swapped with another one of the same kind, so everything stays obtainable
- Chaos mode: anything from anywhere
- One seed per world, saved in the world and shared with every player in multiplayer
- Coherent stack sizes, protected progression items, everything configurable

Commands: /rseed (show or change the world seed), /rdebug

Do not enable it together with tRandomizer.
