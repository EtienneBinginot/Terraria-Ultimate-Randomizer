==[Ultimate Randomizer]==
A complete randomizer that shuffles nearly everything: loot, chests, crafting, shops, enemies, their stats and where they live. Every world gets its own seed, the randomness follows your progression, and with the default settings everything you need stays findable.

Originally made for the YouTube videos of Pootouf & Nouke: https://www.youtube.com/@pounouke

==[What gets randomized]==
- Enemy and boss drops, plus extra random drops on every enemy (loot abundance x3 by default), critters included
- Treasure bags, crates, chests, pots, fishing, mined ores, shaken trees, herbs, the Extractinator, the Angler's rewards
- Jackpots: now and then a drop aims a few tiers ahead of your progression
- Recipe results, ingredients and crafting stations, planned so that everything can still be crafted
- Shimmer, shops, the starting kit, the arrival order of town NPCs
- Enemy spawns redistributed between the biomes
- Enemy and boss stats on a bell curve, giants and minis (damage stays within x3: defense is subtracted from it)
- Adaptive power: gear far ahead of the world makes enemies tougher, mostly in health

==[Modes]==
- Coherent (default): items keep their category and tier and unlock as you defeat bosses
- Guaranteed shuffle: every item is swapped with another of the same category and tier
- Chaos: anything from anywhere

Progression items are protected; basic stations, boss summons and needed ingredients are guaranteed on common enemies, in pots and wooden chests.

==[Codex and hints]==
J key: drops of the enemies you killed, bags you opened, craft tree, enemies of each biome. The Guide sells hints telling you where to find an item.

==[Settings and multiplayer]==
Everything is configurable (English and French). The seed is saved with the world and shared in multiplayer.
Commands: /rseed (show or change the seed), /rjournal, /rchests (older worlds), /rdebug

Do not enable it together with tRandomizer.
