==[Ultimate Randomizer]==
A complete, configurable randomizer for Terraria, tied to each world.

Features:
- Random enemy and boss drops with vanilla-like drop tables (random number of drops, vanilla-like rates), treasure bags, crates and goodie bags
- Random shops (value-based prices), recipes (same category and tier, value guard), shimmer transmutations
- Random chests, fishing, mined ores and pot loot
- Random enemy spawns (same tier, same movement), random arrival order of town NPCs
- Boss and enemy stat multipliers, random starting kit
- Coherent mode: items keep their category and tier, and unlock as you defeat bosses (no Zenith from the first slime... unless you are very lucky)
- Guaranteed shuffle mode: every item is swapped with another one of the same kind, so everything stays obtainable
- Chaos mode: anything from anywhere
- One seed per world, saved in the world and shared with every player in multiplayer
- Coherent stack sizes, protected progression items, everything configurable (English and French)
- Discovery journal (J key): drops of the monsters you killed and content of the bags you opened

Commands: /rseed (show or change the world seed), /rjournal, /rchests (older worlds), /rdebug

Do not enable it together with tRandomizer.
