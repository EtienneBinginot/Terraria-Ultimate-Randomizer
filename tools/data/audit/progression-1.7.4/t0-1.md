# Progression t0-1 (start to Brain of Cthulhu, Crimson world, seed 1119453773, v1.7.4)

Verdict: OK (small delays only). Every requirement of tiers 0 and 1 has a planned recipe or source at tier 0 or 1, no export row
contradicts another (I re-checked: every ingredient of a recipe doable at tier <=2 is backed by a finding tier, a shop or a recipe of
its claimed tier; doable = max of ingredients; no recipe is doable earlier than its ingredients or stations allow). Nothing here
needs a pickaxe above 50, and a 50+ pickaxe is craftable at tier 0. The costs are chains of 3-4 hops and herb luck (40 % kept).

## Path per tier
World ore map (worldsources.tsv): copper TILE gives Platinum ore, lead tile gives COPPER ore, platinum tile gives LEAD ore, silver
tile gives Silver. Iron, tin, gold, tungsten, demonite tiles are absent (Crimson, and the other pairs picked by the world): Iron ore
never exists, so bars come from planner recipes. Start inventory is vanilla (copper pick 35, axe, sword) plus the random kit.

Tier 0 stations (all from start materials, all doable 0):
- Work Bench R903: 4 Gel + 1 Daybloom, by hand. Pacing log: "First work bench ... by hand at tier 0", guardian Fourmilion 12.5 %.
- Table: Wooden Table R902 (7 Clay + Daybloom @WorkBenches) or Dynasty Table R1579 (1 Lead ore @WB). Chair: Sandstone Chair R1336 (3 Gel @WB).
- Sawmill R1514: 2 Fallen Star + 5 Slush Block (snow biome) + 5 Wood @WB. Guardian: Zombie 12.5 % (t0/t1).
- Furnace R1990: 5 Acorn (KeepAcorns) + Daybloom + 7 Gel + 1 Copper ore + 6 Clay @**Sawmill**. Guardian: Sand Slime (t1) 12.5 %.
- Cooking Pot R2113: 23 Silver ore @Tables. Iron Anvil R2077: 3 Lead ore @CookingPots. Guardians: Spore Bat 12.5 % (pot), Jungle Bat t1 (anvil).
- Bottle R155 (1 Stone @Furnaces), Loom R1503 (1 Blinkroot @Bottles), Keg R1501, Bookcase R907, Heavy Work Bench R2105, Glass Kiln R2106: all d0.
- Bars (Furnaces): Iron Bar R2076 = 1 Lead ore + 1 Silver ore (also R1427 5 Blinkroot @Bottles); Copper Bar R2050 = Iron Bar; Platinum Bar R2160 = 8 Lead ore @Tables.
- Tinkerer's Workshop R3065 (Deathweed x4 + Armored Cavefish x5 @Anvils): d1, guardian Piranha. Not required to progress.

Pickaxes / mining chain: copper pick (start, 35) mines every tier-0 ore. Needed powers: Meteorite 50, Crimtane/Obsidian 55, Hellstone 65.
- 50+: Tungsten Pickaxe R2131 (5 Gel + 5 Iron Bar + 4 Silver ore @Anvils) d0; Platinum Pickaxe R2161 (59, Glass Wall x3, Amber x5, Iron Bar x4 @Tables) d0.
- 55: Fossil Pickaxe R1575 (d0, but 1 Lead Bar, a long chain), Gold Pickaxe R1710 (d1, @IceMachine), Candy Cane Pickaxe (55, guaranteed in pots from tier 1, worldsources.tsv).
- Crimtane ore has no recipe use in this world (no recipe takes CrimtaneOre); it only drops (EoC 30-90, BoC 40-90, bags). No mining need.
- 65 (Hellstone) belongs to tier 2: Nightmare Pickaxe R2187 (10 Shadow Scale) d2.

Weapons / armor / healing at tier 0: 32 melee, 23 ranged, 8 magic, 66 armor, 43 tool recipes are doable at tier 0 (examples: Copper Shortsword
3 Lens @Furnaces, Shadewood Sword 1 Blinkroot @Kegs, Tin/Copper armor sets, Gold Bow). Healing: Lesser Healing Potion R161 (Mining Potion +
2 Pumpkin + 6 Daybloom @Chairs), Healing Potion R163 (Trapsight Potion R935 + 5 Snow Block @Bottles), Mushrooms; Merchant sells no healing before hardmode.

Eye of Cthulhu: Suspicious Looking Eye R2912 = 8 Copper ore @Demon/Crimson Altar (altar is a world station, tier 0). Also guaranteed 2.5 % on
Jungle Bat / Spore Skeleton (t1 hosts). Natural night spawn needs >200 max life (Life Crystal also R2987: 10 Emerald @Chairs).
King Slime: Slime Crown R2921 = 30 Copper ore @Altar (R2920: Fish Bowl + 10 Blinkroot); also 3.75 % on 10 slime kinds (t0/t1).
Brain of Cthulhu: Bloody Spine R2918 = 1 Dirt Bomb + 23 Copper ore @Altar. Dirt Bomb R329 = 7 Shiverthorn (no station). Also 1.33 % guaranteed
on Cave Bat, Ice Slime, Lava Slime, Skeleton, Demon Eye. Alternative: break the Crimson Hearts with any hammer (see below).
Absent-evil handling is in place (Eater of Worlds loot is left out of the permutation in a Crimson world).

Crimtane Bar R2191 = 1 Purple Mucos (100 % from the Demon Eye, shop t0) + 1 Bunny Stew (R100: Moonglow + Dirt @WB) @Furnaces, d1.
Demonite Bar R2179 = 1 Crimtane Bar @Tables. Tissue Sample: see Questions; every recipe using it is doable at tier 4 only.
King Slime / EoC / BoC loot (bags kept vanilla, tables permuted): nothing needed for the next tier is hidden in them.

## Big delays (all minor, 1-2 hours of early game at worst)
1. Furnace needs a Sawmill (R1990 @Sawmill): the chain Work Bench -> Sawmill (2 Fallen Stars, 5 Slush from the snow biome) -> Furnace is
   the gate to bottles, looms, all bars, hence to the Anvil (via Tables -> Cooking Pot 23 Silver ore, no furnace) and tools. Guardian
   drops (12.5 % on Zombie for the Sawmill, only a Sand Slime for the Furnace) soften it. Fix if wanted: keep critical stations' own
   stations among the critical ones (a Furnace at WorkBenches).
2. Anvils group: only Iron Anvil is practical (Lead Anvil R2090 needs 3 Grasshopper Statues R2042: 10 Moonglow, 5 Amber Stone, 3 Green Thread @HeavyWB).
   23 Silver ore for the Cooking Pot is the largest raw cost of the tier (silver tiles are cavern depth).
3. Herb luck: Daybloom, Blinkroot, Shiverthorn, Moonglow are kept only 40 % of harvests (Blinkroot never keeps one of the 3 replacements). The
   Bloody Spine path needs 7 Shiverthorn (about 18 harvests) in the snow biome; the hearts path avoids it.
4. Hammers (hearts, and nothing else here needs one): Wooden Hammer R1524 = 7 Pink Gel (Moonglow 20 % per harvest, SkeletonTopHat rare, or R3985
   3 Bottled Honey @CookingPots): bad. Better: Shadewood Hammer (17 Ice Block @CookingPots), Copper Hammer R2053 (10 Silk @Anvils; Silk sold by
   the Demolitionist at 17 silver each), Platinum Hammer (9 Diamond @CookingPots). Fine, but the obvious hammer is the worst one.
5. Rope has no shop and a heavy shell recipe R3203 (Vertebrae + Bamboo + Gel + Green Paint @Anvils): exploration is harder, not blocked.
6. Bombs (tier 2 availability: Hoplite 72 %, Skeleton Merchant shop t2, herb, R3003) do not exist before the Brain, so hearts cannot be exploded early.

## Questions
- Tissue Sample (tier 2 item, findability 4): no Classic-mode drop row at all (BoC's Classic table gives KS loot + Crimtane + Bloody Machete); only the
  Expert bag (20-40, kept) and the shell recipe R3277 (Blue Jellyfish + Greater Mana Potion @Anvils, avail 4). Harmless now (all 16 recipes using it
  are d4+), but tell me if a Classic world is meant to find it at tier 2.
- Crimtane Ore is orphaned (no recipe takes it; Crimtane Bar no longer needs ore, R2191). Intended by the ingredient shuffle? No effect on progress.
- Mining of Crimstone is listed at 65 power by the mod (RecipePlanner.VanillaTilePick); the Crimson chasm with the Hearts is reachable
  through open caves at 35, I did not verify any wall that forces 65. Worth a quick in-game look in the chasm.
- Hive (76 recipes at d0/1, many armor sets x25) is a tier 0 "world" item although the Jungle only opens at tier 1 for guaranteed items
  (GuaranteedSources.AllowedTier): walking there is possible at tier 0, just far.
