# Progression t2-3 (after the evil boss -> Wall of Flesh beaten)
Verdict: OK, with soft spots only on optional routes. No softlock found in this band: the pickaxe 65/100 path, the Hellforge,
both keys, the Guide Voodoo Doll, Skeletron access and the Wall of Flesh prerequisites are all reachable at their tier.
Crimson world (Brain of Cthulhu is the evil boss, the Eater of Worlds is absent). R = recipes.tsv index.

## Key finding that simplifies the band
No recipe in the export uses an ore item (Hellstone, Meteorite, Cobalt..Titanium, Chlorophyte) as an ingredient: the bar recipes
were rebuilt. Hellstone Bar = Bomb x7 at Tables (R2271), Meteorite Bar = Bomb x6 at Furnaces (R2226), hardmode bars come from
Soul of Light / Pixie Dust / Black Thread / Tissue Sample (R2292, 2305, 2318, 2346, 2360). So pickaxe power only gates the loot of
tiles, never a recipe. Ore map (worldsources.tsv): Hellstone -> Hellstone, Meteorite -> Meteorite (identity, vanilla power 65 and
50); the first hardmode pair (Cobalt/Palladium tile, power 100) drops Titanium/Adamantite items, which nothing needs.

## Path per tier
### Tier 2 (PostEvilBoss)
- Underworld access: digging down, no item. Ash Blocks drop as Ash (TileDropFeature only remaps ore items), so the Underworld is
  also the bomb factory: Bomb = AshBlock x2 at Bookcases (R3003); AshBlock also R3006 (Lava Bucket + Stake + 2 Glowstick at
  Furnaces). Bombs are also sold by the Skeleton Merchant (tier 2, 547 cp), dropped 72 % by Hoplite (marble) and the Fireblossom
  herb gives Stinger / Bomb / Lava Bucket.
- Hellforge (tier 2): AshBlock x8 at Anvils (R3020); also a 12.5 % guaranteed drop on Hornets. Fine.
- Hellstone mining (power 65). Pickaxes with power >= 65 at tier <= 3: Nightmare (65), Deathbringer (70), Molten (100).
  - Molten Pickaxe R2275: MeteoriteBar x20 at Bookcases (doable tier 2). Each bar = 6 Bombs -> 120 Bombs = 240 Ash Blocks + 20 bar
    crafts. Many crafts but trivial materials (ash is mined by the stack). Best route.
  - Nightmare Pickaxe R2187: ShadowScale x10 at Furnaces. In a Crimson world there is no Eater of Worlds: scales come from the
    Dryad (Blood Moon + Crimson, 690 cp, shop tier 3-4) and crates (1.3-3.5 %). Deerclops drops a Nightmare Pickaxe 100 % (extra,
    boss and bag) -> optional second route.
  - Deathbringer Pickaxe R2199: FlarefinKoi x10 + Umbrella + Stinger x10 at Anvils. Flarefin Koi = 3 Combat Books + 2 Crimtane Bars
    (R3749) or crates 1.8-4 %; Combat Book only from crates (25 % dungeon/jungle crates) and shops -> impractical (delays).
- Stations for that chain are all tier 0-1: Furnace R1990 (Sawmill; Daybloom from Bone-throwing Skeleton 50 % / Sea Turtle 35 %),
  Bookcase R907 (Sawmill; guardian HeadacheSkeleton 12.5 %), Anvil R2077 (Lead Ore x3 at Cooking Pot; guardian Jungle Bat).
- Molten gear (not required): helmet R2279 MeteoriteBar x9 at Anvils; breastplate R2280 CyanHusk x10 (R3238 Bomb + Sake at
  Furnaces, or Bone-throwing Skeleton 2 drops it 100 %); greaves R2281 Fireblossom x10 at Anvils.
- Queen Bee (optional): Abeemination craftable at tier 0 (R2919) and guaranteed 1.33 %/kill on 6 hosts (tier 1). Her loot is
  kept/permuted cosmetics and weapons, nothing progression-critical.
- Deerclops (optional): Deer Thing R231/232 at the Demon Altar (tier 1) + the same 6 hosts at 1.33 %.
- Evil-boss leftovers: Tissue Sample 20-40 and Crimtane 40-90 come from the Brain itself (bag, kept).
### Tier 3 (PostSkeletron)
- Skeletron access: the Old Man talks at night at the dungeon (town NPC, never swapped: EnemySwap skips town NPCs and the Dungeon
  Guardian is excluded). No item needed.
- Skeletron drops: bag kept (Skeletron Hand, Book of Skulls, Breaker Blade, Demon Heart, 5 gold) + permuted trophies/masks and
  extras. Nothing needed to progress.
- Golden Key: R3058 LesionBlock + Fireblossom + Lava Bucket at Kegs (doable tier 2), also 1.5 % drops (Snow Flinx, Angry Bones
  Muscle, Frozen Zombie, Crawdad) and Demolitionist shop (tier 5). Shadow Key: guaranteed 3.8 % on 6 hornet/Anomura hosts (tiers
  1-4), Santa shop; no recipe. Both findable by tier 2-3 (findability.tsv).
- Guide Voodoo Doll (WoF summon): Protection.NeededFrom = PostEvilBoss. Sources: Voodoo Demon 100 % (Underworld), Dilated Eye /
  Demon Eye (-40) 6 % (common night mobs, tier 1), Slimed Zombie 6 %, guaranteed 2.7 % on Angry Bones Big (Helmet t2, Big t3)
  and Granite Golem. Plenty.
- Imbuing Station R3343 (AntlionMandible x4 + Fireblossom x6 at Tables) and guardian Giant Flying Antlion 12.5 % (tier 3). Alchemy
  Table R3964 has no consumer recipe in the export. Bone Welder R3706 / guardian Pantless Skeleton.
### Wall of Flesh fight (tier 4 gate)
- Needs: Guide alive, the doll, a way down (hellevator), healing: Healing Potion R163 (Trapsight Potion + 5 Snow Blocks at Bottles,
  doable tier 0; Merchant also sells it), Lesser Healing R161, Obsidian Skin R170 (doable tier 1). Nothing is gated beyond tier 3.
  Drops: Pwnhammer 100 % (boss and bag, kept) plus guaranteed hosts at tier 4 as a net.
- After the WoF the Molten Pickaxe (100) already mines the Cobalt/Palladium pair. For the next pairs (power 110/150) the cheap
  pickaxes are Orichalcum Pickaxe R2339 SpiderFang x10 at Tinkerer's Workbench (11 drop sources) and Titanium Pickaxe R2367
  PixieDust x10 at Mythril Anvil. Cobalt Pickaxe R726 needs 10 Turtle Shells (pirate ship / hard crates / Pearlstone Bricks at
  Adamantite Forge): tier 4 band.

## Blockers
None.

## Big delays
1. Deathbringer Pickaxe (R2199) is out of practical reach before late tier 3: 10 Flarefin Koi (30 Combat Books or ~10 lucky crates)
   + Umbrella + 10 Stingers. Harmless: Molten (tier 2) and Deerclops' Nightmare Pickaxe cover the 65+ need.
2. Molten Pickaxe costs 120 Bombs (240 Ash Blocks): acceptable, but it is the only always-available 65+ route in a Crimson world
   (Nightmare needs Shadow Scales, see Questions).
3. Fireblossom (x6 Imbuing Station recipe, x10 Molten Greaves, 1 per Golden Key, x2 Mythril Brick R730): the herb gives
   Stinger/Bomb/Lava Bucket instead, the Painter sells it only in shop tiers 0-1 and the Demolitionist only with a condition item
   in the inventory. Reliable route: R3049 = Lava Bucket + Stinger + Meteor Shot (one Meteor Shot craft R254 = 70 shots from 2
   Meteorite Bars + 30 Stakes; Stakes: Crawdad 100 % extra, Skeleton Merchant 27 cp). Doable at tier 2, some Stake farming; the
   Imbuing Station itself is not critical thanks to its guardian drop.

## Questions
1. Shadow Scale in a Crimson world: items.tsv gives it tier 2 / avail 2 from the absent Eater of Worlds, so ~25 recipes (Nightmare
   Pickaxe R2187, Shadow Greaves R2186, Necro Breastplate R2255, Molten Fury R2273, Luck Potion R215, dyes...) are marked doable at
   tier 2 although only the Blood Moon Dryad slot and rare crates give scales. BossPermutation.AbsentEvil is used by BiomeSlots and
   the loot permutation only (grep), not by RecipePlanner/Findability. None of those recipes gates progress, but a critical recipe
   (station/key) rebuilt with Shadow Scales in a Crimson world would be silently harder (same for Tissue Sample in a Corruption
   world). Suggest treating the absent evil's drops as unavailable in the ingredient availability pass.
2. Findability ignores boss treasure bags: BrainOfCthulhuBossBag / SkeletronBossBag have findability "never", so Tissue Sample shows
   tier 4 (only crates counted) although the Brain bag gives 20-40 at tier 2. Export artefact only, but recipes using Tissue Sample
   (R2360 Titanium Bar, R3277...) are then planned at tier 4. Intended?
3. Town NPC arrival, replicated from TownNpcOrderFeature with seed 1119453773 (assuming that is SeedSystem.WorldSeed; my own
   Python port of DetRandom.Hash and System.Random, not verified in game): Demolitionist = 50 silver, Nurse = 2 NPCs + life,
   Steampunker = bullet item, Merchant = first boss, Pirate = explosive item, Painter = Skeletron, WitchDoctor = dye item,
   Dryad = 8 town NPCs, Arms Dealer = 14 NPCs, Clothier = 10 % bestiary, Dye Trader = Queen Bee, Party Girl = mech boss,
   Cyborg = pirates, Bestiary Girl = Plantera. So the Merchant (Healing Potion, Lava Bucket) comes only after the Eye of Cthulhu
   and the Dryad (Shadow Scales at Blood Moon) only with 8 NPCs. shops.tsv ignores arrival times, so every "first shop tier" is
   optimistic; worth confirming with the debug panel.
4. Voodoo Demon drops the doll with chance 1 (kind permuted): if that is real, the doll is trivial; otherwise the 6 % night mob
   drops and the guaranteed hosts still carry it.
