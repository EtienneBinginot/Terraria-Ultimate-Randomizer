# Progression t4-5 (hardmode start to the three mechanical bosses), Crimson world, seed 1119453773, v1.7.4

Verdict: OK with delays. No softlock found. The two real gates (Pwnhammer, the three mechanical summons) have direct
drops from hardmode enemies that spawn at tier 4. What is slow or odd: Mythril Anvil / Adamantite Forge, every pick
power of 200, and the hardmode ores, which no recipe uses at all in this world.

## Blockers
None found for the progression itself (Pwnhammer -> altars -> hardmode ores; 3 summons -> Hallowed Bars -> Plantera).

## Big delays
1. **Mythril Anvil and Adamantite Forge are slow stations**: the anvil costs ~42 gold of shop items (8 x AncientBattleArmorMaterial), the forge
   depends on one Crimson-only hardmode enemy (BloodFeeder, 12.5 %) because its recipe is circular (TitaniumBar needs the forge) and asks 14 Tin Ore
   in a tin-less world. 63 + 31 recipes of the band need them. Suggested fix: the station recipe rebuilder should not pick an ingredient that can only be made
   at the same station (cycle) nor an absent-ore item (TinOre has no mining tile here, avail 1 only from WoodenCrate 3.6 %).
2. **Pick 200**: only PickaxeAxe is realistic (needs a Queen Slime kill, 14 town NPCs for the Party Girl, 15 Soul of Light).
   Drax needs 5 License Dog (crate only), Chlorophyte Pickaxe needs 10 Broken Bat Wing.
3. **Soul of Light** is the scarce soul in this world (global rule, Skeleton Merchant, one crate); CobaltBar (#2292, 3x for PickaxeAxe) uses 5 each.
