# Progression t6-7 (Plantera to Golem, optional bosses of the period)
Verdict: OK. No blocker found. Every item needed to reach and kill Plantera, open the Temple, summon Golem and reach the
Cultist stage has a source at its tier (guaranteed or 100 % boss drop), and the optional event/boss summons are craftable
or guaranteed. Only soft delays (Ectoplasm is rare-ish, the 200 pick power at tier 5 has no cheap route). Spawn maps of the
hosts (BiomeSlots) cannot be checked from the export; see Questions.

## Path per tier
Tier 5 -> 6 (kill Plantera)
- Plantera Bulb: world tile, grows once the 3 mechs are down; no item, no mod rule touches it (no PlanteraBulb in Common/).
- Pick power 200 (Chlorophyte ore; `PickGates`). NOT a progression gate here: ChlorophyteBar is recipe #2415 = HallowedBar x3
  at HeavyWorkBench (ore not used), and no gate item needs ChlorophyteOre. Ore exists in the world (worldsources: Chlorophyte
  unchanged, present True). Pick-200 options at tier 5 (items.tsv, recipes.tsv):
  - ChlorophyteDrill #392: SoulofSight x10 (Twins/bags, 100 % 15-30 on Retinazer/Spazmatism) + LightKey x3 (#233, SoulofFlight x10 each; shop tier 5). Cheapest.
  - Drax #2407: SoulofMight x3 (Destroyer 100 % 25-40) + LicenseDog x5 + Actuator x6 (#3169 SoulofLight+SoulofFlight). LicenseDog only from IronCrateHard (33 % of the crate) / Santa (tiers 1-3) = the slow part.
  - ChlorophytePickaxe #2422: BrokenBatWing x10 (each = FireFeather + 3 SoulofSight + Wire, #3418) + HallowedBar x14 + TurtleShell x8; or MushiLadybug 8 % extra drop; Merchant sells it at tier 6 only (318794 cp).
  - PickaxeAxe #2408 (HallowedBar x13, SoulofMight x10, CrystalNinja shirt, Minecart kit). Spectre Pickaxe / Shroomite Claw at tier 6.
- Hallowed Bar: Twins/Prime 100 % x15-30 each + bags 20-35. Plentiful.

Tier 6 -> 7 (Golem)
- Temple Key (tier 6): Plantera drops it 100 % (drops.tsv Plantera idx 11, permuted from itself, lock 0) and Plantera Bag (kept). Also guaranteed 2.9 % on FungoFish/IlluminantBat/CursedHammer/HoppinJack (findability tier 5, Temple excluded as host biome). Jungle Temple door opens; Lihzahrd bricks stay unbreakable without pick 210 (Picksaw = Golem drop), so the key is the only entry: it is covered twice.
- Lihzahrd Power Cell (Golem summon, tier 6, findability 5): guaranteed 7.9 % on GiantFlyingFox and Arapaima (non-guaranteed route 2 % LihzahrdCrawler, 2 % DesertGhoulHallow). ~13 kills per cell. No recipe needed.
- Lihzahrd Altar: world tile in the Temple, item "never" is irrelevant.
- Golem: bag kept, drops 1/7 each of the vanilla items, Picksaw 25 %, extra Ichor Flask. Its Beetle Husks moved to HallowBoss (see below).

Tier 7 -> 8 (Cultist): no item needed (Cultist appears after Golem; Celestial Sigil is tier 8, guaranteed on CultistBoss).

Optional bosses / events of the period
- Duke Fishron (Truffle Worm, tier 4, "WorldSummon"): guaranteed 6.25 % on 6 hosts (PossessedArmor, IceTortoise, RedDevil spawn from tier 4; RustyArmoredBonesFlail, BlueArmoredBones, NecromancerArmored from 6). Fishing pole: WoodFishingPole #recipe tier 0 (GreyStucco x5), Reinforced pole tier 0. OK.
- Empress of Light (Prismatic Lacewing / EmpressButterfly, tier 6): guaranteed 8.4 % on BloodJelly (spawn 4), Lihzahrd (temple, 6), HellArmoredBonesSword (6). No other source. OK.
- Pumpkin Moon Medallion #2927: Ectoplasm x18, LightShard x10, TungstenBar x3, SoulofSight x3 at Furnaces (doable 6). ~28 Ectoplasm.
- Naughty Present (Frost Moon) #2928: Ectoplasm x5, FlaskofVenom x3 (Nailhead 100 % 2-3, Merchant shop), LunarTabletFragment x4 (SkeletonArcher 14 %, Mechanic shop), Diamond x5, TinkerersWorkbench. OK.
- Solar Tablet (Eclipse) #2929: ShroomiteBar x2 at MythrilAnvil. ShroomiteBar #2440: LightShard x5 + SpookyWoodWall x4 at CrystalBall (CrystalBall: tier 4 station, GiantBat guardian 12.5 %, Clothier tier 5). One bar ~ 7 Ectoplasm + 5 Soul of Light + 2 EmptyBullet. Eclipse is optional. OK.
- Spectre Bar #2447: Ectoplasm x3 + LightShard x3 -> 2 bars (Kegs). Spectre Pickaxe #2452 = SpectreBar x3 + TinOre x21 (Loom).
- Beetle Husk: HallowBoss 100 % x4-8 (permuted from Golem) and recipe shell #3713 (CursedFlame x2, Nanites, Ectoplasm, FlaskofNanites, ImbuingStation; doable 7). Shop tier 7. Not needed to progress. OK.
- Autohammer (station): recipe #3430 Ectoplasm x7 + BlackFairyDust x3 (Arms Dealer/Demolitionist shops tier 6, ~23 gold each; guardian FungoFish). Only used by cosmetic/lunar-vanity recipes. LihzahrdFurnace (doable 7, CursedFlame x3 + ClusterRocketII) only cosmetic + fragment-from-husk recipes (band 8).

Ectoplasm (the material of this band, tier 6, hard gate)
- Enemy: DungeonSpirit only (100 %, 1-2; it is a rare spawn: BiomeSlots.ExplicitRare, not flagged as child spawn). Crate DungeonFishingCrateHard 1.2 %. Witch Doctor shop at tier 6 only (55056 cp each, "roll" slot). Shell recipe #3408: Eggnog x3 at Tables; Eggnog drops 40 % x1-2 from ManEater and 60 % x2-3 from GiantFlyingFox (jungle), and #3632 GoldWorm.
- LightShard availability 6 (sources RuneWizard 6.7 %, BigMimicCrimson 4 %, IceMimic 10 %, crimson crate 1.8 %): craftable #3123 = Ectoplasm + SoulofLight.

## Blockers
None.

## Big delays
- Ectoplasm supply is the bottleneck of the whole band (Medallion 28, one Shroomite bar 7, Shroomite Digging Claw 28, Sigil 30 (t8), Autohammer 7). Reliable routes are the Eggnog craft (3 Eggnog = 1 Ecto, ~1.5 Eggnog per GiantFlyingFox kill, ~2 fox kills per Ectoplasm) and the Witch Doctor at tier 6 (5.5 gold each) if he is in town; the Dungeon Spirit is rare. Acceptable, but a full Shroomite set (about 45 bars) would be roughly 300 Ectoplasm. Optional gear only.
- Pick power 200 at tier 5 has no cheap route except ChlorophyteDrill (needs ~30 Souls of Flight + 10 Souls of Sight); Drax is held back by 5 LicenseDog (crate-only after tier 3). Harmless since the ore is not a gate here, but the mod's `PickGates` still treats 200 as a gate (Chlorophyte bar/ore recipes do not exist in this plan).
- Beetle Husk only 100 % from the Empress (needs the Lacewing, 8.4 % on 3 hosts) or the shell recipe; optional.

## Questions
1. Host spawn maps: TempleKey/PowerCell/Lacewing/Truffle Worm hosts (CursedHammer, GiantFlyingFox, Arapaima, BloodJelly...) are placed by GuaranteedSources on the BiomeSlots map; the export has no spawn biome per host, so I could not confirm that CursedHammer (vanilla Corruption, world is Crimson) is reachable. Not a risk for the key (Plantera 100 %) but for PowerCell the two hosts GiantFlyingFox + Arapaima carry all the guarantee: worth a run of the existing `TempleKeyOutsideTemple` / guarantee tests (or checking that both hosts have a slot in a biome reachable at tier 5).
2. DungeonSpirit: vanilla spawn is a natural rare dungeon spawn after Plantera; if BiomeSlots moves its slot out of the Dungeon, Ectoplasm falls back on the Eggnog craft and the shop only. Confirm its slot biome (Dungeon expected).
3. ChlorophytePickaxe "WallofFleshEye" source appears in findability.tsv (2 enemy sources) but has no row in drops.tsv for the pickaxe (only MushiLadybug 8 %); probably a global/vanilla rule, harmless.
