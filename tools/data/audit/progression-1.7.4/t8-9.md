# Progression t8-9 (Lunatic Cultist to Moon Lord and after) + cross-check of all summons and stations
Verdict: OK. No blocker in tiers 8-9: the Cultist spawn is untouched, the Ancient Manipulator and the Celestial Sigil are
guaranteed Cultist drops, pillar fragments are kept as vanilla, Luminite comes from Moon Lord (core and bag) and every
tier 8-9 recipe is doable at its own tier. The all-tier cross-check of boss summons and progression stations also finds
no blocker (a few mild delays and questions below).

## Path per tier
### Tier 8 (PostCultist)
- Cultist spawn: dungeon Devotee / Archer Blue / Archer White are in the EnemySwap exclusion list
  (Common/Features/EnemySwapFeature.cs l.69), so the ritual is vanilla (Golem down, dungeon entrance). No item needed.
- Lunatic Cultist (CultistBoss, tier 8) drops, classic and expert, chance 1:
  - LunarCraftingStation (Ancient Manipulator): a permuted row AND a `guaranteed` row (drops.tsv, owner CultistBoss).
  - CelestialSigil: `guaranteed` row (also craftable, recipe 2930 at LunarCraftingStation, Vortex Fragment x21 + Ectoplasm x30,
    tier 8 doable, but it is a rematch tool only).
  - Fallback if the single Cultist kill's station is lost: recipe 4102 (AdamantiteForge, Nebula Fragment x3, Conveyor Belt x3;
    doable tier 8); shops Santa 8, Wizard/Cyborg 9 also roll it.
- Four pillars (LunarTower*, tier 8, `kept`): each drops its own fragment 12-60 (expert 24-100), chance 1, plus an extra
  gear item. Pillar enemies are never swapped. Fragments are also craftable from each other at the Manipulator (recipes
  2462, 2472, 2482, 2492, all doable 8), so one lopsided drop cannot lock anything.
- Moon Lord spawn: Sigil (guaranteed from the Cultist) or the vanilla pillars-down countdown. Vortex x21 + Ectoplasm x30
  for a rematch Sigil: Ectoplasm comes from DungeonSpirit (chance 1, 1-2) and the Witch Doctor shop (tier 6, 55056 cp).
- Fragment crafted items (furniture, platforms): all have doable tier 8, station Manipulator or common tiles.
### Tier 9 (PostMoonLord)
- Luminite (LunarOre): MoonLordCore 70-90 chance 1 (classic and expert), MoonLordBossBag 90-110 (bags.tsv idx 3); shell
  recipe 4095 (ImbuingStation) is an alternative. Painter sells it only at Christmas (29872 cp).
- Luminite Bar (rec 2457, LunarCraftingStation): Luminite x3 + Apple Pie x2 (tier 9 doable). Apple Pie: vanilla recipe is
  gone (shell 4241 needs Cosmic Ember Brick); sold by the Merchant (snow-biome house condition, 61681 cp) with an added
  guaranteed slot at the Pirate (arrival tier 0 in this world), also Moon Lord head/hand x2 chance 1, Pirate Corsair 0.7 %.
  So a bar costs ~3 luminite + ~12 gold of pies.
- Luminite gear: Luminite tools/armor/wings need 3 bars each (2457 users: 2464-2499, 2540, 2542), i.e. 9 Luminite + 6 pies
  each. One kill (70-90 core + 90-110 bag = 160-200 Luminite) covers roughly 15-20 pieces. Fine.
- Fragment gear (Solar Flare / Vortex / Nebula / Stardust armor and weapons) is tier 9 and doable at 9.

## Big delays
- Lihzahrd Furnace: tier 6 item, recipe 3707 only doable at 7 (Cursed Flame x3 avail 7, Cluster Rocket II avail 6, at
  AdamantiteForge). Findability 7, but shop tier 6 and the temple/guardian likely give it at 6. The 16-19 recipes using it at
  tier 6-8 (Lihzahrd bed/candle, Echo Monolith, many Solar/Vortex pieces) are therefore 0-1 tier late. Mild.
- Cursed Flame / Dark Shard avail 7 / 8 although tier 4 (single 1% style sources: Cursed Flame from AngryTrapper,
  PirateCrossbower, BlackRecluseWall; Dark Shard from Pirate Corsair, Crossbow, Parrot, Big Mimic Corruption): Dark Shard is also
  an ingredient of several tier 8 furniture pieces (avail 8) and has a shop from tier 5. Furniture only, not progression.
- Modded Magic Storage ingredients in tier 9 gear: Vortex Pickaxe (2477) and Stardust Helmet (2494) need
  UpgradeBlueChlorophyte x11 each; the only cheap source is the Cyborg shop (102666 cp each, ~113 gold for 11; unit recipe 2970
  needs Beetle Husk x7 + Ectoplasm x7 per piece). Solar Flare Breastplate / Hamaxe need Radiant Jewel x2 (Moon Lord core 10 %,
  bag 18-25 % in expert/master; or recipe 4645 from a Portal Gun) - several kills in classic. Not needed to progress.
- Celestial Sigil rematch: the Cultist can be killed once, pillars once, so Vortex Fragment x21 later relies on leftover
  fragments or Furnace/Manipulator fragment conversions (recipes 2462-2492, circular but seeded by pillar drops). Fine if the
  player keeps a few fragments. Rematch is optional.
- Other cross-tier station delays (mild, not progression): 18 Lesion Station and 18 Flesh Cloning Vat furniture recipes of tier 2
  doable at 4; Bone Welder recipes of tier 0 doable at 2.

## Questions
- Apple Pie is the only vanilla-style food needed for every Luminite Bar. Its availability relies on the Merchant's
  snow-biome condition or the Pirate slot (shops.tsv ApplePie, "added slot at Pirate"); if the player cannot house the
  Pirate (drops.tsv Pirate Corsair 0.7 %, Moon Lord x2 per kill) bars would be limited to Moon Lord drops. The export lists only
  the Merchant row, not the Pirate row: please confirm that the Pirate shop really has the added slot at tier 4+.
- Corruptor / DesertGhoulCorruption (Apple Pie sources) do not exist in a Crimson world; harmless since the shop covers it.
