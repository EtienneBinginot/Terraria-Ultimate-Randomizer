[h1]Ultimate Randomizer[/h1]
We wanted a randomizer where every new world feels like a different game, without ever getting stuck. So this mod shuffles almost everything: what enemies drop, what's in chests, recipes, shops, which monsters live where, and how strong they are. It still follows your progression, and with the default settings everything you need can be found.

We made it for our YouTube videos ([b]Pootouf & Nouke[/b]): [url=https://www.youtube.com/@pounouke]youtube.com/@pounouke[/url]

[h2]What changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Loot.[/b] Enemy and boss drops are shuffled between enemies of the same tier, and every enemy has a couple of extra random drops on top. Critters drop things too. Treasure bags, crates, chests, pots, fishing, ores, shaken trees, herbs, the Extractinator and the Angler are all randomized.
[*][b]Jackpots.[/b] Now and then a kill drops something a few tiers ahead of where you are, with a flash and a sound. Expect about one every half hour early on. A few chests in each world are jackpot chests.
[*][b]Crafting.[/b] Recipes get new ingredients, and most of them a different crafting station. Everything is planned so it all stays craftable. Shimmer is shuffled as well.
[*][b]Shops and town.[/b] Shops sell random items at prices based on their value. You start with a random kit, and town NPCs move in in a random order.
[*][b]Enemies.[/b] Monsters are spread over different biomes. Their health, damage, defense and size vary, sometimes a lot: watch out for giants (and the tiny minis). Bosses get random health and damage too. Damage can't go past x3, so it stays fair.
[*][b]Adaptive power.[/b] If you get lucky and find gear far ahead of the world, enemies get tougher (mostly more health), so the run stays fun instead of trivial.
[/list]

[h2]Three modes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Coherent[/b] (default): items keep their type and tier, and unlock as you beat bosses.
[*][b]Guaranteed shuffle:[/b] every item is swapped with another one of the same type and tier.
[*][b]Chaos:[/b] anything can come from anywhere. Good luck.
[/list]

[h2]You won't get stuck[/h2]
Boss summons, pickaxes and the materials you need are always dropped by the common enemies of a biome you can reach at that point. Crafting stations are little quests: each one has its own random recipe, and one enemy also carries it as a backup. In Expert and Master, a boss's normal-mode drops come in its treasure bag, as usual.

[h2]Codex and hints[/h2]
Press [b]J[/b] to open the codex: what each enemy you killed can drop, what was in the bags you opened, the crafting tree and the enemies of every biome. If you're lost, the Guide sells hints for crafting stations, pickaxes and boss summons. A hint shows the whole path to the item, from the enemy or ore down to the last crafting step. The Bestiary and Recipe Browser show this world's drops.

[h2]Settings and multiplayer[/h2]
Nearly everything can be tuned in the mod settings (in English and French). Each world has its own seed, saved with it and shared in multiplayer. Other mods' items, recipes and enemies are mixed in too, on a best-effort basis; Magic Storage is supported.

Commands: [b]/rseed[/b] shows or changes the seed, [b]/rjournal[/b] opens the codex, [b]/rchests[/b] randomizes the chests of a world created before you installed the mod.

Don't enable it together with tRandomizer.
