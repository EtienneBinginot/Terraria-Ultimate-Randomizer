[h1]Ultimate Randomizer[/h1]
A randomizer that makes every world play differently while keeping the game completable. It shuffles almost everything: enemy drops, chest contents, recipes, shops, enemy spawns and enemy stats. The randomness follows progression, and with the default settings every required item can be found.

Originally made for the YouTube videos of [b]Pootouf & Nouke[/b]: [url=https://www.youtube.com/@pounouke]youtube.com/@pounouke[/url]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Loot.[/b] Enemy and boss drops are shuffled between enemies of the same tier, and every enemy gets a few extra random drops. Critters can drop items as well. Treasure bags, crates, chests, pots, fishing, ores, shaken trees, herbs, the Extractinator and the Angler rewards are randomized too.
[*][b]Jackpots.[/b] Any kill has a small chance to drop an item a few tiers above current progression, with a visual and sound effect (about one every 30 minutes early in the game). Each world also contains a few jackpot chests.
[*][b]Crafting.[/b] Recipes get new ingredients, and most of them a different crafting station. All recipes are planned to remain craftable. Shimmer transmutations are randomized as well.
[*][b]Shops and town.[/b] Shops sell random items, priced from their value. The starting kit and the arrival order of town NPCs are randomized.
[*][b]Enemies.[/b] Enemies are redistributed between biomes. Health, damage, defense and size vary from one enemy to the next, with occasional giants and minis. Boss health and damage are randomized too. Total damage is capped at x3.
[*][b]Adaptive power.[/b] Gear found far ahead of the world's progression makes enemies tougher, mostly through extra health, so that a lucky early find does not trivialize the game.
[/list]

[h2]Modes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Coherent[/b] (default): items keep their category and tier, and unlock as bosses are defeated.
[*][b]Guaranteed shuffle:[/b] every item is swapped with another item of the same category and tier.
[*][b]Chaos:[/b] any item can come from any source.
[/list]

[h2]Progression safety[/h2]
Boss summons, pickaxes and required materials are always dropped by common enemies of a biome reachable at that stage. Each crafting station has its own randomized recipe, and one enemy also carries it as a backup. In Expert and Master mode, a boss's normal-mode drops are in its treasure bag, as in vanilla.

[h2]Codex and hints[/h2]
The codex ([b]J[/b] key) lists the possible drops of every enemy killed, the contents of opened bags, the crafting tree and the enemies of each biome. The Guide sells hints for crafting stations, pickaxes and boss summons; each hint shows the full path to the item, from the enemy or ore to the last crafting step. The Bestiary and Recipe Browser show the world's randomized drops.

[h2]Settings and multiplayer[/h2]
Nearly every system can be configured in the mod settings, available in English and French. Each world has its own seed, saved with the world and shared in multiplayer. Content from other mods is included on a best-effort basis, and Magic Storage is supported.

Commands:
[list]
[*][b]/rseed[/b]: show or change the world's seed
[*][b]/rjournal[/b]: open the codex
[*][b]/rchests[/b]: randomize the chests of a world created without the mod
[/list]

Not compatible with tRandomizer: do not enable both.
