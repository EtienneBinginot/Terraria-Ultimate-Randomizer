[h1]Ultimate Randomizer[/h1]
A complete randomizer that shuffles nearly everything: loot, chests, crafting, shops, enemies, their stats and where they live. Every world gets its own seed, the randomness follows your progression, and with the default settings everything you need stays findable.

Originally made for the YouTube videos of [b]Pootouf & Nouke[/b]: [url=https://www.youtube.com/@pounouke]youtube.com/@pounouke[/url]

[h2]What gets randomized[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Loot:[/b] enemy and boss drops (shuffled between enemies of the same tier by default), plus extra random drops on every enemy (loot abundance x3 by default), critters included. Treasure bags, crates, chests, pots, fishing, mined ores, shaken trees, herbs, the Extractinator, the Angler's rewards.
[*][b]Jackpots:[/b] every kill has a small chance of a jackpot (about one every 30 minutes early on): an item a few tiers ahead of your progression, with light and sound. Jackpot chests too.
[*][b]Crafting:[/b] recipe results, ingredients and crafting stations, planned so that everything can still be crafted. Shimmer transmutations too.
[*][b]Economy and town:[/b] shops (value-based prices), a random starting kit, the arrival order of town NPCs.
[*][b]Enemies:[/b] spawns redistributed between the biomes, stats on a bell curve, giants and minis, random boss health and damage. Damage stays within x3 in total, because defense is subtracted from it.
[*][b]Adaptive power:[/b] gear far ahead of the world makes enemies tougher (mostly their health), so a lucky early weapon stays fun.
[/list]

[h2]Modes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Coherent[/b] (default): items keep their category and tier and unlock as you defeat bosses.
[*][b]Guaranteed shuffle:[/b] every item is swapped with another of the same category and tier.
[*][b]Chaos:[/b] anything from anywhere.
[/list]
Progression items are protected. Boss summons, progression tools and the ingredients you need are guaranteed on the common enemies of a biome of their tier. Crafting stations are small quests: a randomized recipe for their tier, and one guardian enemy as a backup.

[h2]Codex and hints[/h2]
Press [b]J[/b]: drops of the enemies you killed, bags you opened, craft tree, enemies of each biome. The Guide sells hints about crafting stations, pickaxes and boss summons (Crafting and Bosses tabs, with a search box). For a crafted item, the hint gives the whole tree: every ingredient and station, down to the enemy that drops it or the ore to mine.

[h2]Settings and multiplayer[/h2]
Everything is configurable, in English and French. The seed is saved with the world and shared in multiplayer.
Commands: /rseed (show or change the seed), /rjournal, /rchests (worlds created before the mod).
Do not enable it together with tRandomizer.
